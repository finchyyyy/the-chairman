# THESE NOTES ARE INTENDED TO BE READ IN PLAINTEXT MODE.
Mao is a game that has existed for far longer than I have. This is my vision of Mao.


    ** ALL RULES, EVEN THE BASIC ONES, CAN BE BROKEN FOR A PRICE
Sometimes, players can just choose to pick up cards and look at them when they shouldn't.
Sometimes, players can play out of turn.
Sometimes, players can [insert something else that completes the rule of three].

I want to live in a world where this is not (necessarily) a bad thing.
My intent for Mao is that there is nothing that you cannot do, as long as you pay for it.
In fact, I want to create opportunities where messing with the natural order of the game is sometimes the *right* thing to do to. 


    ** ONE CARD A TURN, EVERY TURN
The Basic Rules of Mao are intended to be a slight extension of UNO or other games.
Under only the Basic Rules, you may only end your turn within 1 card of what you start with:

-1: you played a card this turn
+0: you both drew and played a card this turn
+1: you drew a card this turn.

Therefore, if you end your turn with 1 card left, the call of "Mao" is a signal that says "I can win the game next turn."


    ** TWO CARDS A TURN IS DANGEROUS
An effect that lets you remove 2 cards from your hand in a given turn is the most dangerous sort of effect in Mao. **Above all else, IT CANNOT BE EASY.**

- Why is it dangerous?
First, it breaks the signal of "Mao" - you need not have to call "Mao" to be able to win (2 cards -> 0 cards on your turn).
Second, it means that the card on the top of the Play Pile can change drastically in the course of a single turn.
    You can force someone into a suit your opponent doesn't want much easier, or just get all your cards in a suit out quickly.
Third, two cards is usually a very large chunk of your hand, and getting rid of it all at once is a very powerful effect.

However, these effects are not necessarily bad, they just need to be closely watched.

The most common rule that a new player will make is this:
"You may play pairs of cards."
I HATE THIS RULE I HATE THIS RULE I HATE THIS RULE.
