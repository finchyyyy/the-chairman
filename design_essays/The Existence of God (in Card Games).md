A good litmus test is this:
	If you have an effect which would allow you to search your deck for a card of a specific type
	 and move it somewhere hidden, should you have to prove that the card is of that type?




Using this definition, we can now define a Godly card game:

	A Godly card game is a card game that relies upon a higher power to enforce its rules.

	Inversely, a Godless card game is one that relies on its players to enforce its rules.
